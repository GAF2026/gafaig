# PURPOSE

This document defines the canonical Organization Submission Contract for GAFAIG (Global Authority for AI Governance).

The Organization Submission Contract establishes the minimum information required for an organization to participate in GAFAIG governance, verification, certification, publication, and registry workflows.

This contract exists to ensure:

* low-friction applicant onboarding
* global applicability
* organization-centric governance participation
* evidence-centric governance verification
* certification continuity
* publication continuity
* registry integrity
* governance scalability

The contract intentionally minimizes collection of unnecessary organizational information.

GAFAIG collects only information required to:

* identify the applicant
* scope governance review
* verify governance evidence
* issue certification outcomes
* maintain registry integrity

GAFAIG does not collect information solely because it may be useful.

---

# CONSTITUTIONAL INTAKE DOCTRINE

All submission fields must be classified as:

* REQUIRED
* OPTIONAL
* CONDITIONAL

Definitions:

REQUIRED

Must exist for every applicant organization.

OPTIONAL

May be supplied when available but is not required.

CONDITIONAL

Required only when applicable to the organization, AI system, governance program, or certification scope.

Applicants may submit applications without OPTIONAL fields.

Applicants may submit applications without CONDITIONAL fields unless the triggering condition exists.

# EVIDENCE-CENTRIC GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLE

GAFAIG operates as evidence-centric governance infrastructure.

The primary certification input is:

Evidence

not:

* tax records
* corporate ownership structures
* executive rosters
* employee counts
* revenue disclosures

Organizations are evaluated based on governance evidence, governance controls, governance practices, governance outcomes, and governance verification results.
