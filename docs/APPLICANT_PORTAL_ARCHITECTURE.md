# Last Updated: 2026-05-19

# PURPOSE

This document defines the canonical Applicant Portal Architecture for GAFAIG.

The Applicant Portal Architecture establishes controlled operational access infrastructure for organizations interacting with GAFAIG governance systems.

Applicant infrastructure exists to support:

* application continuity
* organization identity continuity
* governance lifecycle visibility
* certification continuity visibility
* publication election workflows
* recertification continuity
* governance communication continuity
* future continuous governance monitoring access

Applicant infrastructure operates beneath deterministic governance authority infrastructure.

Applicant infrastructure is NOT authoritative governance infrastructure.

---

# MOST IMPORTANT GOVERNANCE RULE

Applicant infrastructure may never directly mutate governance authority.

All authoritative governance state must continue to originate exclusively from deterministic governance authority infrastructure.

Applicant systems may facilitate workflows and visibility,
but may never directly:

* certify organizations
* publish registry records
* revoke governance authority
* mutate registry authority
* mutate verification authority
* alter deterministic governance state

without canonical Layer 1 execution paths.
